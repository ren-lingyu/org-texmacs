* Scalars and elementary expressions
Inline notation can contain indices (math (concat "a" (rsub "n"))), powers
(math (concat "x" (rsup "2"))) and Greek letters (math "<alpha>+<beta>").

#+NAME: quadratic
#+begin_equation
(concat "x=" (frac (concat "-b<pm>" (sqrt (concat "b" (rsup "2") "-4ac"))) "2a"))
#+end_equation

Equation [[quadratic]] illustrates a radical nested inside a fraction.
Brackets grow with their contents:

#+begin_equation*
(around* "(" (frac (concat "1+" (sqrt "5")) "2") ")")
#+end_equation*

* Sums, products and integrals
#+begin_equation
(concat (big "sum") (rsub "k=1") (rsup "n") "k=" (frac (concat "n" (around* "(" "n+1" ")")) "2"))
#+end_equation

#+begin_equation*
(concat (big "prod") (rsub "k=1") (rsup "n") "k=n!")
#+end_equation*

#+begin_equation
(concat (big "int") (rsub "0") (rsup "1") "x" (rsup "2") " d x=" (frac "1" "3"))
#+end_equation

The limit and derivative can be written with native subscripts and fractions:

#+begin_equation*
(concat "lim" (rsub "h<rightarrow>0") (frac (concat "f" (around* "(" "x+h" ")") "-f" (around* "(" "x" ")")) "h") "=f'" (around* "(" "x" ")"))
#+end_equation*

* Matrices and aligned rows
#+begin_equation
(concat "A=" (matrix (tformat (table (row (cell "1") (cell "2")) (row (cell "3") (cell "4"))))))
#+end_equation

* A short argument
#+NAME: norm-definition
#+CAPTION: The Euclidean norm
#+begin_statement definition
For a vector (math (concat "v=" (around* "(" "x,y" ")"))), define
(math (concat "N" (around* "(" "v" ")") "=" (sqrt (concat "x" (rsup "2") "+y" (rsup "2"))))).
#+end_statement

#+NAME: norm-positive
#+CAPTION: Non-negativity
#+begin_statement theorem
The quantity in Definition [[norm-definition]] is nonnegative and vanishes
exactly at the zero vector.
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement proof
Each square is nonnegative. Their sum is zero exactly when both coordinates
are zero, and the nonnegative square root preserves this condition.

#+begin_equation*
(concat "x" (rsup "2") "+y" (rsup "2") "=0 <Longleftrightarrow> x=y=0")
#+end_equation*
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement lemma
For any real scalar (math "c"), the norm is homogeneous:
(math (concat "N" (around* "(" "cv" ")") "=|c|N" (around* "(" "v" ")"))).
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement remark
Theorem [[norm-positive]] has one reference identity for the whole statement.
Caption and body remain separate native structures. This collection does not
introduce separately numbered rows inside one equation array.
#+end_statement

#+end_statement
