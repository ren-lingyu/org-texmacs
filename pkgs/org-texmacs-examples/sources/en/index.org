* Reading the collection
Org is the canonical source. Ordinary *strong text*, /emphasis/, =literal code=
and inline mathematics such as (math (concat "x=" (frac "1" "2"))) can share
a paragraph. A named note can be cited twice[fn:source][fn:source]. An anonymous
note can contain mathematics[fn::For instance, (math (sqrt "2")) is irrational.].

Read about TeXmacs at [[https://www.texmacs.org][the TeXmacs project]].
