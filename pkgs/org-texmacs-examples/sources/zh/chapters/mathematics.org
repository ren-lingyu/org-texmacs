* 标量与初等表达式
行内公式可以包含下标 (math (concat "a" (rsub "n"))), 幂次
(math (concat "x" (rsup "2"))) 和希腊字母 (math "<alpha>+<beta>").

#+NAME: quadratic
#+begin_equation
(concat "x=" (frac (concat "-b<pm>" (sqrt (concat "b" (rsup "2") "-4ac"))) "2a"))
#+end_equation

公式 [[quadratic]] 展示了分式内部的根式. 括号也可以随内容伸展:

#+begin_equation*
(around* "(" (frac (concat "1+" (sqrt "5")) "2") ")")
#+end_equation*

#+end_equation

#+begin_equation

利用原生下标和分式, 还可以写出极限与导数的定义:

#+begin_equation*
(concat "lim" (rsub "h<rightarrow>0") (frac (concat "f" (around* "(" "x+h" ")") "-f" (around* "(" "x" ")")) "h") "=f'" (around* "(" "x" ")"))
#+end_equation*

* 矩阵与多行公式
#+begin_equation
(concat "A=" (matrix (tformat (table (row (cell "1") (cell "2")) (row (cell "3") (cell "4"))))))
#+end_equation

* 一个简短的论证
#+NAME: norm-definition
#+CAPTION: 欧几里得范数
#+begin_statement definition
对向量 (math (concat "v=" (around* "(" "x,y" ")"))), 定义
(math (concat "N" (around* "(" "v" ")") "=" (sqrt (concat "x" (rsup "2") "+y" (rsup "2"))))).
#+end_statement

#+NAME: norm-positive
#+CAPTION: 非负性
#+begin_statement theorem
定义 [[norm-definition]] 中的量非负, 并且仅在零向量处取零.
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement proof
每个平方项均非负, 因此平方和为零当且仅当两个坐标都为零.
取非负平方根不会改变这一条件.

#+begin_equation*
(concat "x" (rsup "2") "+y" (rsup "2") "=0 <Longleftrightarrow> x=y=0")
#+end_equation*
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement lemma
对任意实数 (math "c"), 范数满足齐次性:
(math (concat "N" (around* "(" "cv" ")") "=|c|N" (around* "(" "v" ")"))).
#+end_statement

#+begin_statement remark
定理 [[norm-positive]] 的引用标识属于整条陈述. 题注和正文保留为不同的原生结构.
本案例不为一个公式阵列内部的各行引入独立编号.
#+end_statement
